Brief History

Rifuwo's Horse Farm is farm that has different types of horse activities that create enjoyable and memorable experience for people who enjoy outdoor activities and love horses The farm has an area where people can learn about horses, care and can experience horse riding. It aims to introduce people like young people and famalies to the world of horses in enjoyable environments As time goes the website will help Rifuwo's Horse Farm reach more customers and provide more information about our service

Mission Statement

Welcome to Rifuwo's Horse Farm

Our mission is to provide a safe, enjoyable and memorable horse-riding experience while encouraging people to care for horses

Vision Statement

Our vision is to become the most popular horse riding farm where people can connect with horses and nature
